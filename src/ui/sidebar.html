Profiles

Each profile has its own tabs, slop tags and logins. Switch with the dots at the bottom. AI agents lets tools like Claude browse as that profile through canbec's filters: add canbec --mcp as an MCP server. Best on its own profile without your logins.

Privacy

Fresh browser on every start

Cookies, storage and cache are forgotten when you quit, and every site sees a slightly different fingerprint. Trackers can't recognise you next time.

Sites ask before they use your camera or microphone; canbec doesn't save the answers.

Privacy tools

Adult content

Always blocked

There is no switch for this, on purpose. The list updates every week and works offline too.

Family DNS blocks adult sites in every app on this computer, not only here.

AI slop

Share your tags as a text file, or import someone else's. To follow a list, put its link into slop-lists.txt in the config folder.

AI text hints

Optional: a small model on this computer guesses whether a page's text reads like AI. Off by default, and a ~90 MB download. Pages never leave your computer. It's often wrong on short or edited text, so it only shows a quiet hint when it's fairly sure.

Search

DuckDuckGo, AI features off

New tabs and searches use noai.duckduckgo.com. Results first seen after 2023 get an obsolete badge, so human-written pages are easy to pick.

Downloads

Files go to your Downloads folder. canbec keeps no download history.

Colours

Colours come from theme.css in the config folder. Changes show after a restart.

Extensions
